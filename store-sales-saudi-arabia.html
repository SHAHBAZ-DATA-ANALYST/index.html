BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS¶

1. Strengthen the Online Channel¶

Online sales generate the majority of revenue. The business should continue investing in digital marketing, website performance, customer retention, and online conversion strategies while monitoring online sales KPIs regularly.

2. Expand Women's Fashion¶

Women's Fashion is the highest-performing product category. The business should maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products and use targeted promotions and cross-selling to increase category revenue.

3. Focus on High-Performing Cities¶

Riyadh is the strongest city by sales. The business should identify the factors driving Riyadh's performance and apply successful strategies to lower-performing cities.

4. Improve Store Sales¶

Store sales are substantially lower than online sales. The business should investigate store-level performance, product availability, promotions, and customer behavior to identify opportunities to increase physical-store revenue.

5. Protect Customer Satisfaction¶

The largest customer-satisfaction group is "High," which indicates a strong positive customer experience. The business should maintain service quality while investigating the customers in the Low and Very Low satisfaction groups to identify areas for improvement.

6. Monitor Yearly Sales Performance¶

Sales were relatively stable from 2020 to 2023, with 2022 recording the highest annual sales. Management should monitor monthly and yearly trends to identify periods of growth or decline and adjust marketing, inventory, and sales strategies accordingly.
