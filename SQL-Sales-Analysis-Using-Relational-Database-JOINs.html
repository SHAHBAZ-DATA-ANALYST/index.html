CONCLUSION¶

This project demonstrates how SQL JOINs can be used to combine data from multiple related tables and transform it into meaningful business analysis. The dataset was checked for missing values, duplicates, unexpected values, and data inconsistencies before being imported into SQL Server. The validation checks showed no NULL values, duplicates, or identified inconsistencies, making the data suitable for analysis.

Using INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN, and RIGHT JOIN, customer, order, order-detail, product, and regional information was connected to create a complete view of sales transactions.

The sales analysis produced several key findings:

  • Bhubaneswar, Odisha generated the highest sales at 1,552,200, while Delhi generated the lowest at 226,200.
  • Female customers generated 4,188,600 in sales compared with 4,048,200 from male customers.
  • Accessories was the highest-selling category with 2,870,400 in sales, while Electronics generated 499,200.
  • April 2025 recorded the highest monthly sales at 1,834,560, while June 2025 recorded the lowest at 541,580.
  • Among the days of the week, Wednesday recorded the highest sales at 1,234,792, while Saturday recorded the lowest at 1,148,992.

Overall, the project demonstrates the practical use of SQL JOINs, aggregation, GROUP BY, date functions, conditional sorting, and data visualization to analyze sales performance from relational datasets. It shows how multiple normalized tables can be connected and analyzed to identify sales patterns across locations, customer segments, product categories, months, and weekdays.

RECOMMENDATIONS¶

Based on the sales analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

  1. Focus on High-Performing Cities and States

    • Increase marketing efforts and inventory levels in top-performing cities and states to maximize revenue.
    • Study the characteristics of these locations to replicate their success in lower-performing areas.

  2. Promote High-Selling Product Categories

    • Allocate more stock and promotional campaigns to categories generating the highest sales.
    • Introduce bundle offers and discounts for popular products.

  3. Improve Low-Performing Categories

    • Analyze customer preferences, pricing, and competition for categories with lower sales.
    • Consider targeted promotions to increase demand.

  4. Optimize Monthly Sales Performance

    • Since sales vary by month, create seasonal marketing strategies and inventory planning.
    • Increase promotional activities before high-sales periods.

  5. Utilize Day-Wise Sales Trends

    • Allocate staff, inventory, and marketing budgets according to high-performing weekdays.
    • Launch special offers on low-performing days to improve sales.

  6. Strengthen Customer Relationship Management

    • Identify top customers and implement loyalty programs to increase repeat purchases.
    • Analyze customer purchasing behavior for personalized marketing campaigns.

  7. Regional Business Expansion

    • Invest more resources in high-performing regions while investigating the reasons for lower sales in other areas.

  8. Future Analysis

    • Extend the project by adding profit analysis, customer segmentation, and top products by region using advanced SQL functions such as RANK() and PARTITION BY.
